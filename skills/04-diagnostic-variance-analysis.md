# SKILL: Diagnostic Variance Analysis

## Trigger / When to Use
Use when investigating budget vs. actual performance, cost overruns, or operational margin shifts.

## Instructions
1. **Calculate Variance Bridge**:
   - **Price Variance**: `(Actual Price - Budget Price) * Actual Volume`
   - **Volume Variance**: `(Actual Volume - Budget Volume) * Budget Price`
   - **Mix / FX Variance**: Isolate product mix shifts and exchange rate fluctuations.
2. **Identify Primary Drivers**: Rank top 3 contributing factors to the variance.
3. **Root-Cause Investigation**: Cross-reference operational metrics (e.g., supply chain delays, headcount growth) to explain the numerical shift.

## Constraints
- Mathematical total of price + volume + mix variance must equal 100% of net variance.
